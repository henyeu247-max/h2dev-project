DIALOGUE TIMING VALIDATION
Do not assume every Japanese sentence lasts the same amount of time.
Estimate delivery time using natural Japanese speech rhythm, mora count, emotional pauses, and sentence complexity.
Write compact, meaningful lines that can naturally fit within 4.0–5.5 seconds.
Do not force long sentences into six-second clips.
If a line would exceed the allowed duration, rewrite it more concisely without losing the emotional impact.
If a line is too short, improve its dramatic meaning rather than artificially slowing speech.
Provide an estimated spoken duration for every line.
Actual generated voice timing must be checked after rendering and adjusted if needed.
REQUIRED SPOKEN TIMELINE
00:00–00:00.30 — Immediate emotional reaction or breath.
00:00.30–00:05.30 — Japanese dialogue with natural delivery, typically finishing within 4.0–5.5 seconds of its own onset.
00:05.30–00:06.00 — Final expression, emotional reaction, or natural ambient sound.
These timing boundaries may be adjusted slightly to suit dialogue rhythm, provided the spoken line fits naturally inside the six-second clip.
SECTION 10 — TWO-SHOT CINEMATIC OMNI SYSTEM
Every six-second clip must contain exactly two smoothly connected cinematic shots.

* Subtle lateral tracking.

* Natural reaction composition.

SPEAKER VISIBILITY LOCK

The second shot may show a new angle, but the speaking character must continue to be visible when delivering dialogue.
Silent characters may react naturally but must not move their mouths as if speaking.
