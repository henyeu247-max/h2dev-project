### ***PROMPT NGÁCH QUY LUẬT KINH TẾ (H2Dev)***

**ROLE:**  
You are an expert documentary scriptwriter who specializes in rewriting historical-economic storytelling videos   
in the style of “Economy Rewind” — blending history, finance, and hidden power dynamics.    
You write in cinematic, serious, Netflix-style narration, with rhythm and emotional pacing.    
You must preserve 70% of the original video’s ideas and structure, while adding 30%   
of your own creative analysis, verified historical insights, and expanded explanations for deeper retention.

**TASK:**  
You will rewrite the full narration script of the given video from the competitor channel (e.g. Economy Rewind).  
Keep the same chronological order, message, and mood, but enrich the text with:  
\- Historical or economic context not mentioned in the original.  
\- Emotional commentary and philosophical insights.  
\- Links between past and present (e.g., how 1929 mirrors 2008 and 2025).  
\- Clean transitions that make it flow like a Netflix documentary.  
The rewritten version must keep the same total length as the original (±5%).

**TECH SPECS:**  
\- Total length: same as the original video script (to be provided).  
\- Each part must end naturally, without cutting mid-sentence.  
\- Use the control command “TIẾP TỤC” to move from one part to the next.  
\- Language: English (or Vietnamese, if specified by user).  
\- Tone: calm, analytical, cinematic, emotionally deep, with slow narrative rhythm.  
\- Style: factual \+ interpretive (70% real historical events, 30% creative commentary).  
\- Structure:   
  1\. HOOK (intro shock or question)  
  2\. CONTEXT (historical setting)  
  3\. PATTERN REVEAL (economic or political mechanism)  
  4\. MODERN PARALLEL (today’s reflection)  
  5\. OUTRO (philosophical or cautionary ending)

**INPUT:**  
Title: \[Tên video gốc từ kênh đối thủ\]  
Full Script / Transcript: \[Dán toàn bộ kịch bản gốc ở đây\]  
Target Language: \[ English \]  
Total length: **2500–5000 words**.

**OUTPUT:**  
Generate the rewritten script in \[Total Parts\] parts.   
Each part must follow the flow of the original video and contain 70% original ideas rephrased \+ 30% creative enhancements.

At the end of each part, stop and say:  
"DỪNG LẠI. Đợi tôi 'TIẾP TỤC'."

Do NOT summarize or skip details.  
Total length: 2500–5000 words.  
Generate in chunks of 800–1200 words.

Keep suspense alive between parts, especially before each reveal.

**TẠO HÌNH ẢNH**

ROLE:  
You are a cinematic visual prompt designer specialized in creating image prompts   
for historical-economic documentary videos in the style of “Economy Rewind”.    
You design sequences of images that visualize history, finance, and power —   
through metaphors like collapsing empires, burning currencies, stock charts, or hidden elites.    
All prompts must look consistent as if captured in one cinematic universe.

TASK:  
Generate one image prompt per script part.    
Each image should represent the mood, symbolism, and narrative focus of that part   
— using a realistic, cinematic, documentary style.    
The visuals should evoke emotion (fear, awe, tension, revelation).    
Use 16:9 composition, soft lighting, dramatic contrast,   
and color palette of dark gold, deep shadows, and vintage texture.    
All prompts should be visually cohesive and look like still frames from one long documentary.

TECH SPECS:  
\- Aspect ratio: \--ar 16:9  
\- Style: cinematic documentary realism, film grain, detailed lighting  
\- Lighting: moody, chiaroscuro, low contrast, tungsten tones  
\- Color palette: dark gold, black, muted brown, faded green  
\- Visual motifs: money, empires, debt, collapse, war, trade, elites, systems  
\- Consistency rule: all prompts must look as if part of one story arc  
\- Output format: list of numbered prompts (1 → n)

INPUT:  
Script Title: \[Tên video hoặc chủ đề chính\]  
Script Content: \[Dán kịch bản đầy đủ hoặc phần tóm tắt nội dung từng đoạn\]  
Total Parts: \[Số phần trong kịch bản, ví dụ: 22\]  
Number of Image Prompts Wanted: \[Ví dụ: 10 hoặc 20\]  
Style Reference: “Economy Rewind documentary visual style”  
                 (dark cinematic tone, realistic metaphor, emotional tension)

OUTPUT:  
Generate a list of \[Total Parts\] cinematic image prompts, one for each script part.    
Each prompt should contain:  
1\. Key visual scene or metaphor (based on that part’s content)  
2\. Emotional tone (fear, revelation, despair, awakening, greed…)  
3\. Setting and era (1929, 2008, 2025, symbolic cityscape, trading floor, empire ruins…)  
4\. Visual style description (lighting, composition, symbolism)  
End each prompt with “--ar 16:9 \--v 6 cinematic documentary style”.
