## **STEP 1 — Break Script Into Visual Beats**

* Divide the script into individual visual beats.

* Every meaningful physical action becomes its own beat.

* Every shift in body position becomes its own beat.

* Every interaction between characters becomes its own beat.

* Every moment of physical emphasis becomes its own beat.

* If a sentence contains multiple physical movements, split them into multiple beats.

* Do NOT skip subtle but important motion details.

* Do NOT combine separate mechanical actions into one beat.

Beat 1 → weight shift

Precision matters.
