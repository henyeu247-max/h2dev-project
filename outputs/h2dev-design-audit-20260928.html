Verified working parts — protect these

Availability & identity

  • 140/140 lesson routes, thumbnails and one-byte media ranges respond as expected.
  • 142/142 local document entries respond; 15 remote-only catalog entries are not missing files.
  • Pagination covers all 140 videos, 156 raw records and 152 visible channels without duplicate identities.
  • 13 dead channels remain intentionally hidden out of 165.

Shell & bounded layout

  • No horizontal root overflow in the 96 measured route/width combinations.
  • At 1920px: index and learn content 1120px; player main 1024px. Unlimited stretching is contradicted.
  • 3 principal shells, 4 learning views, 3 player views, 6 content dialogs and one drawer are identified.
  • Baseline source checks pass and shell check passes 6/6; behavioral gaps still remain.

Claims corrected before execution

Not established by source patterns

  • Missing image attributes do not prove CLS; dimensions may already come from CSS.
  • Eight CSS requests do not prove FOUC or poor FCP.
  • A timeupdate event is not fixed at four events/second.
  • No measured basis for “3× faster”, “0.01ms lookup”, “15KB API”, “2000 DOM operations/s” or battery claims.
  • The limited contrast check found no failure in 82 simple-background gray-text elements; one complex case was excluded.

Data & safety corrections

  • Master DB merges benchmark and raw channel populations; different counts are not automatic data corruption.
  • 156 raw records represent 149 unique channels. Voice coverage needs identity mapping before gap arithmetic.
  • HEAD 200/Range 206 proves availability, not full media or subtitle integrity.
  • No PNG/media/backup deletion is authorized by this review.
  • Database rebuilding is destructive; it must never be run as a harmless audit.

Standards correction: the project uses 44px targets. WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 (AA) is 24×24 CSS px with exceptions; SC 2.5.5 (AAA) is 44×44 CSS px with exceptions. A sub-44 visual box is not automatically an AA failure.
